# TaskNotes 4.16.1-fork.9

Fork build of TaskNotes 4.16.1 for BRAT.

## Added

- (#2537) In the natural language field, Backspace right after a highlighted word or phrase turns it back into plain title text. For example, "May 1" in "Plan the May 1 launch" can stay in the title instead of setting a date. The next Backspace deletes as usual. See [Highlighting in Natural Language Input](https://tasknotes.dev/features/task-management/#highlighting-in-natural-language-input).
