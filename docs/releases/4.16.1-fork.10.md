# TaskNotes 4.16.1-fork.10

Fork build of TaskNotes 4.16.1 for BRAT.

## Added

- (#2538) Tasks can be marked with `bundle: true` to say they may share a scheduled block with other tasks, such as a handful of 2-minute tasks. It is a building block for future auto-scheduling; TaskNotes does not schedule tasks with it yet. Turn on **Bundle** in the task modal, or type "bundle" in the natural language field. Turning it off removes the property. See [Bundling Tasks](https://tasknotes.dev/features/task-management/#bundling-tasks).
